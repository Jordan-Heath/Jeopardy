How to Play

  • The team highlighted in yellow picks the question and answers first.
  • Only the highlighted team has to answer. Other teams may answer or pass.
  • Copying the highlighted team's answer (shown in yellow on the other teams' buttons) earns half points if correct, but loses double points if wrong.
  • Correct answers add points, wrong answers subtract points. Highest score wins!
